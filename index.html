How to Register, Login, Deposit & Withdraw on Jai Club

A visual walkthrough of the four most-asked-about Jai Club processes — with real app screenshots taken during our testing.

1 Register / Sign-up

How to register an account on Jai Club

  1. Tap the Register button on the home screen.
  2. Enter your 10-digit Indian mobile number.
  3. Tap Get OTP — a 6-digit code arrives via SMS.
  4. Enter the OTP and create a password (8+ characters with letters and numbers).
  5. Optionally paste an invitation code if you have one.
  6. Tap Register — your account is created instantly.

Note: No email or KYC is required at signup. KYC is triggered only later, when you try to withdraw amounts above roughly ₹5,000.

🎯 Register on Jai Club →

2 Login

How to log in to your Jai Club account

  1. Open the Jai Club app or website.
  2. Tap Login from the home screen.
  3. Enter your registered mobile number.
  4. Enter your password.
  5. Tap Login — you'll land back on the home lobby.

Forgot password? Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen. Recovery is done via OTP on your registered mobile number — keep your SIM active.

→ Open Jai Club Login

3 Deposit (UPI)

How to deposit money into your Jai Club wallet

  1. After login, tap the Wallet icon in the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap Deposit.
  3. Choose a deposit amount — minimum is typically ₹100; most welcome bonuses unlock from ₹300 upwards.
  4. Select a UPI option — the app displays a current VPA (UPI ID) that rotates every few days.
  5. Open PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm or any UPI app and pay to that VPA.
  6. Funds are usually credited to your Jai Club wallet within 2–7 minutes.

Important: The merchant name on your UPI receipt will not say "Jai Club" — it is usually a generic LLP or trading-company name. Screenshot every deposit (UTR, date, amount) so you have proof if there is ever a credit dispute.

4 Withdraw

How to withdraw your winnings from Jai Club

  1. After login, tap Wallet → Withdraw.
  2. First time only: complete KYC — upload PAN card (front + back), a recent bank statement, and sometimes Aadhaar plus a live selfie. KYC typically takes 24–72 hours to verify.
  3. Add your bank account: account number, IFSC code, and account-holder name (must match your registered name exactly).
  4. Enter the withdrawal amount — minimum is usually ₹100.
  5. Tap Confirm — the request enters the payout queue.
  6. Small withdrawals (₹100–₹2,000) usually settle in 4–24 hours; larger withdrawals (₹5,000+) take 24–72 hours after KYC.

Heads-up: Withdrawals above ₹10,000 often trigger manual review and additional document requests, and may be delayed or rejected. Read our full withdrawal test report before depositing large amounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jai Club legal in India?

Jai Club operates in a legal grey area. Real-money games of chance (like Wingo color prediction, dice and Aviator crash games) are explicitly prohibited under state-level gambling laws in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and others. Even where not explicitly banned, the Public Gambling Act, 1867 generally makes operating such games illegal. India's Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY) issued new online-gaming rules in 2023 requiring registered self-regulatory bodies — Jai Club has no public verification of this approval.

Bottom line: Even if downloading the app is not directly criminal in your state, depositing money has no legal protection if the operator absconds, freezes your funds, or rejects withdrawals.

Is Jai Club a scam?

"Scam" is a strong word. Jai Club is a real, functioning app — games run, deposits go in, some withdrawals do come out. But the economic design is built so the operator wins long-term. With a typical return-to-player (RTP) of 90–95%, statistical certainty means players lose money on average. Withdrawal complaints, KYC-stalling and "minimum balance" rules also affect a significant portion of users. We don't call it a scam — but we don't call it fair, either.

Can I really withdraw money from Jai Club?

Yes — many users do successfully withdraw small amounts (₹100–₹2,000). Withdrawals above ₹5,000 often trigger additional KYC verification, processing delays of 24–72 hours, or rejection citing "betting pattern" or "promo abuse." Plan for the possibility that any amount above your initial deposit might never be paid out. Read our detailed withdrawal test results.

Are there real "Jai Club prediction tricks" that work?

No. Every "prediction trick," "hack," "VIP signal channel" or "100% accurate formula" on Telegram, YouTube or WhatsApp is either (a) a scam to sell you a paid channel, (b) a referral funnel to make commission off your losses, or (c) confirmation bias from random wins. The Wingo result is generated by a server-side Random Number Generator (RNG). Past results have zero predictive value for the next result — this is the well-documented independence of probability events. Read our color prediction maths explainer.

What's the minimum deposit on Jai Club?

The advertised minimum deposit is typically ₹100, with most bonuses requiring ₹300 or more. We strongly recommend that if you do choose to play, treat any deposit as entertainment money you've already lost — never deposit anything you can't afford to lose entirely.
