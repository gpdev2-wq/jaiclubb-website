1. What is K3 Lottery?

K3 Lottery on Jai Club is a three-dice prediction game inspired by the centuries-old Chinese game Sic Bo. Every round, three six-sided dice are rolled by the server's random number generator, and the result (three numbers between 1 and 6) determines payouts across multiple bet types. Rounds last 1 minute — 60 rounds per hour — placing K3 in the middle of Jai Club's "speed-of-loss" spectrum: faster than 5D Lottery, slower than Wingo.

2. How a K3 round works

  1. 60-second timer counts down. Betting open for 50 seconds.
  2. You can place multiple bets per round (sum, triple, single, big/small, odd/even).
  3. At T=0, three dice are "rolled" — their values displayed with animation.
  4. All applicable bets settle simultaneously.

3. Every bet type explained

  • Sum (3 to 18): predict the total of all three dice.
  • Big / Small: sum ≥ 11 (Big) or ≤ 10 (Small) — but triples don't count.
  • Odd / Even: sum parity — triples excluded.
  • Triple specific: all three dice the same number (e.g., 555).
  • Any triple: any of the six possible triples comes out.
  • Single number: at least one of the three dice shows your number — pays based on how many.
  • Pair (any double): exactly two dice match.

K3 lottery has a more variable house edge than Wingo — depending on the bet you pick, you're surrendering anywhere from 3.7% to 16.7% per round to the operator. The "exotic" bets (specific sums, triples) have the worst expected value, despite being the most tempting because of the big multipliers.

Common K3 mistake:

Players chase the 200× triple payout because it's "only 7.4% house edge." But the probability is 0.46% — meaning you'll wait an average of 217 rounds to hit one. At ₹100/round, that's ₹21,700 in turnover to expect one ₹20,000 win. The variance bankrupts most chasers long before the payoff arrives.

6. Practical playing advice

If you play K3 anyway, here are the math-optimal choices:

  • Stick to Big/Small or Odd/Even bets — lowest house edge (~4.7%).
  • Avoid the exotic high-payout bets unless you fully accept they're entertainment, not strategy.
  • Set a per-session loss limit (we recommend 20% of bankroll) and walk when you hit it.
  • Don't chase. K3's variance can produce 10+ consecutive losses on a 50/50 bet — that's normal.
