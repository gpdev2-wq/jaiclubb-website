Privacy Policy.

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how Jaiclubb.app ("we", "us", "our", "the site") collects, uses and protects your information when you visit or interact with our website. This site is operated under Indian law and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 applies.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information (optional): if you register, we store your username, email and an encrypted password (stored on your device only in this demo build; replace with a real backend in production).
  • Analytics data: pages you visit, time on page, approximate location (country/state level), browser type, device type, referring URL.
  • Newsletter subscription (optional): if you submit your email, we store it for the purpose of sending you our newsletter.
  • Cookies: functional and analytics cookies as described below.
  • Comments/messages: if you contact us, we keep the email and content for as long as needed to respond.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect any payment information.
  • We do not collect KYC data (PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements).
  • We do not collect health, biometric or other "sensitive personal data" as defined under DPDP Act.

3. How we use your data

  • To operate the website (technical necessity).
  • To improve content based on what readers engage with.
  • To respond to your messages.
  • To send our newsletter (only if you've opted in).
  • To display non-personalised advertisements (see section 5).

4. Cookies

We use:

  • Functional cookies — to remember your preferences (e.g., login state).
  • Analytics cookies — to understand which pages are popular (using services like Google Analytics or Plausible).
  • Advertising cookies — if we display ads via Google AdSense or similar, those services may set their own cookies.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work as well.

5. Advertising

We may display third-party advertisements (e.g., via Google AdSense) to support the site. These services may set their own cookies to display non-personalised ads. We do not allow targeted advertising of gambling products to our users.

To opt out of personalised ads from Google, visit Google Ads Settings.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Hosting providers, analytics providers and email-newsletter providers (data processors)
  • Law enforcement when legally required under Indian law

7. Your rights under DPDP Act 2023

As an Indian data principal, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@jaiclubb.app. We respond within 30 days.

8. Data security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS (TLS encryption), regular software updates and access controls. No system is 100% secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe.

9. Children

This site is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be flagged at the top of this page for 30 days.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@jaiclubb.app
