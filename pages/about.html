About Jaiclubb.app.

An independent, non-commercial educational info site about the Jai Club app and the wider color-prediction gaming ecosystem in India.

What we are

Jaiclubb.app is an independent educational info site. We document how Jai Club and similar real-money gaming apps work, what the actual player experience is, and what the math and legal context look like. We're not a gambling site. We don't host games. We don't accept deposits.

Why we built this

Color prediction apps reached massive scale in India around 2022-2024. Many users — particularly young men in Tier 2/3 cities — were drawn in by promises of easy money and lost amounts ranging from tiny to ruinous. The Hindi-English internet was flooded with promotional "tricks" videos and Telegram "VIP" channels, but balanced, factual, honest articles were rare. We started this site to fill that gap.

Our editorial policy

  • No referral links. We do not earn money when readers sign up to Jai Club or any similar platform.
  • No paid promotion. We do not accept payment from operators in exchange for coverage.
  • Real testing. Every review on this site is based on hands-on testing with real (small) deposits, plus reader interviews and document review.
  • Honest negative coverage. When the verdict is negative, we say so. When something works (small withdrawals do, for example), we acknowledge that too.
  • Educational tone. We explain the maths, the business, and the legal context — not just "is it good or bad."

Who funds this site?

We may run standard, non-personalised display advertising via networks like Google AdSense or similar to cover hosting costs. We do not run referral links to gambling operators. We do not accept sponsored content from operators. If we ever begin to, we will disclose it clearly.

Our team

Jaiclubb.app is run by a small group of Indian writers, developers and consumer-protection researchers. We choose to operate pseudonymously because covering grey-market gaming operators publicly attracts risks we'd rather not invite. We're happy to verify our identities and credentials privately to journalists, academics or law enforcement on request.

Corrections & feedback

If you find something factually wrong, please tell us. We update articles as new information arrives — every page carries a "Last updated" date.

What we are not

  • We are not Jai Club, 91 Club, BDG Win, Daman Games or any similar app
  • We are not an operator, agent or affiliate of any gambling platform
  • We are not lawyers — our legal commentary is informational, not legal advice
  • We are not financial advisors — our coverage of gambling math is educational, not investment guidance
